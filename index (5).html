Questions before you buy

Do I need a licence to buy?

Texas doesn’t require a permit to buy a firearm or ammunition. You do need to be old enough (18 for rifles and shotguns, 21 for handguns) and pass a federal background check for firearms. Laws change, so check current rules.

How does buying a firearm online work?

You pay online and we ship the firearm to a licensed dealer near you. You complete the background check and Form 4473 there, then take it home.

Can ammunition be shipped to my home?

Yes, to buyers of legal age. An adult must sign for the delivery.
